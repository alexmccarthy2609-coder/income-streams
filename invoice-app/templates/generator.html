What does a UK invoice need to include?

A unique invoice number, your name and address, your client's name and address, what you're charging for, when the work was done, the invoice date, the amounts and the total. VAT-registered businesses also need their VAT number and the VAT amount. Read our guide to writing an invoice in the UK.
