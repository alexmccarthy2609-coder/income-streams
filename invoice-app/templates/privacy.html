Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What we store

  • Your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We can't see your password.
  • The business details, clients and invoices you enter, so you can use them again.
  • Your plan (Free or Pro) and the customer number Stripe gives us if you subscribe.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach our servers.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who can see your data

Only you. We don't sell or share your data. It is stored with our hosting provider so the service can run.

Free invoice generator

Invoices made with the free generator (without an account) are turned into a PDF and sent straight back to you. We don't store them.

Your data, your choice
