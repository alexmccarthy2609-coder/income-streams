OfficeKit

Hours of spreadsheet, PDF & file admin, done in seconds with Python.

  • Merge Excel
  • Split PDFs
  • Bulk rename
  • Clean CSVs
  • Scrape tables
$ officekit excel-merge sales_*.xlsx
Wrote 14,382 rows to merged.xlsx

$ officekit pdf-split contract.pdf -r 1-3,9
Wrote 2 files to split/

$ officekit clean-csv leads.csv
2,104 rows in -> 1,877 rows out
(219 duplicates, 8 empty removed)

$ officekit rename scans/*.jpg --apply
Renamed 312 files.
